# Company Posts Related to APAC -- What to Look For

## Goal
Monitor Shopify's official LinkedIn company page and blog for posts that signal APAC focus, investment, or strategic direction.

## High-signal post types
- Posts from Shopify's company page mentioning APAC markets, SEA, Japan, India, ANZ
- Posts authored by APAC leadership (e.g., Shaun Broughton, MD APAC & Japan)
- Product launch announcements scoped to APAC (local payment methods, currency support, language support)
- Merchant spotlights featuring APAC-based brands
- Reports or whitepapers on APAC ecommerce trends published by Shopify

## Medium-signal post types
- Shopify employees posting about relocating to or working from APAC
- Event recaps from APAC conferences
- Job promotion announcements for APAC-based roles
- Partner ecosystem posts mentioning APAC agencies or integrators

## What to filter out
- Global product updates with no APAC-specific angle
- Reposted content from non-Shopify authors with no Shopify commentary
- Generic motivational or culture posts

## Key accounts to monitor
- Shopify company page: https://www.linkedin.com/company/shopify/
- Shaun Broughton (MD, APAC & Japan): https://www.linkedin.com/in/shaunbroughton/
- Shopify blog: https://www.shopify.com/blog
- Shopify news: https://www.shopify.com/news

## Search approach
- WebSearch: "Shopify" + "APAC" OR "Asia Pacific" OR "Southeast Asia" + site:linkedin.com
- WebSearch: site:shopify.com/blog + APAC keywords
- If LinkedIn MCP tools are available: get_linkedin_company_posts for Shopify, then filter for APAC mentions

## Output format
For each relevant post, capture:
- author (company page or individual)
- date
- text_preview (first 200 chars)
- signal_type (product_launch / merchant_spotlight / thought_leadership / hiring / event)
- signal_strength (high / medium / low)
- url
