# News & PR Signals -- What to Look For

## Goal
Surface recent news that indicates Shopify is investing in, partnering within, or expanding across APAC markets.

## High-signal news types
- New office openings or co-working hubs in APAC cities
- Partnerships with APAC-local platforms (Rakuten, Shopee, Lazada, Tokopedia, LINE, Grab)
- Regulatory filings or licenses in new APAC markets
- Executive appointments with APAC scope
- Earnings call mentions of APAC growth or investment
- Acquisitions of APAC-based companies or teams

## Medium-signal news types
- Conference keynotes or sponsorships at APAC events (e.g., Spikes Asia, eTail Asia, ShopTalk)
- Merchant success stories from APAC (signals marketing investment in region)
- Integration announcements with APAC payment providers (PayNow, GrabPay, Paidy, etc.)
- Logistics / fulfillment partnerships in APAC

## Low-signal (note but don't prioritize)
- Generic global announcements that mention APAC as one of many regions
- Third-party analyst reports without Shopify-specific actions
- Community / developer meetups

## Search queries to use
- "Shopify APAC" + current year
- "Shopify Asia Pacific expansion"
- "Shopify Japan partnership"
- "Shopify Singapore"
- "Shopify Southeast Asia"
- "Shopify India ecommerce"
- "Shopify Australia"
- site:shopify.com/news + APAC keywords

## Output format
For each relevant news item, capture:
- headline
- source
- date
- signal_type (partnership / office / executive / earnings / acquisition / event)
- signal_strength (high / medium / low)
- summary (2-3 sentences)
- url
