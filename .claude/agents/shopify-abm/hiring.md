# Hiring Signals -- What to Look For

## Goal
Identify whether Shopify is actively hiring leadership or strategic roles in APAC that indicate expansion intent.

## High-signal roles
- VP / Director / Head of anything in APAC, SEA, Japan, ANZ
- Sales Lead, Country Manager, General Manager for any APAC market
- Marketing leadership: Head of Marketing, Growth Lead, Brand Lead scoped to APAC
- Partnerships / BD roles focused on APAC integrations or local platforms (e.g., Rakuten, Lazada, Shopee)
- Policy / Government Relations roles in APAC (signals regulatory groundwork)

## Medium-signal roles
- Senior engineering roles in APAC time zones (indicates product localization)
- Support / Success roles in local languages (Japanese, Bahasa, Mandarin)
- Localization / Translation roles

## Low-signal (ignore unless volume is high)
- Generic remote roles with no APAC scope
- Internships
- Contractor / freelance postings

## Where to search
- WebSearch: "Shopify hiring [role] [APAC region] site:linkedin.com"
- WebSearch: "Shopify careers APAC" site:shopify.com/careers
- LinkedIn job listings: sg.linkedin.com/jobs/shopify-jobs, jp.linkedin.com, au.linkedin.com

## Output format
For each relevant role found, capture:
- title
- location
- seniority (executive / senior / mid)
- signal_strength (high / medium / low)
- url
- date_posted (if available)
